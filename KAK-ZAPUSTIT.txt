========================================================================
  UMNIK — kak zapustit na drugom kompyutere
========================================================================

1. Raspakuyte arhiv v KOREN diska, naprimer:
      D:\Umnik
   ili E:\Umnik

   Ne kladite v "Dokumenty" i ne v put s russkimi bukvami ili probelami.

2. Esli arhiv skachali iz interneta / oblaka:
   pravoy knopkoy po papke Umnik -> Svoystva ->
   vnizu "Razblokirovat" -> OK
   (inache Windows mozhet blokirovat .bat)

3. Zapustite PROVERKA.bat
   On nichego ne menyaet. Vnizu dolzhno byt: VSYO NA MESTE.

4. Zapustite umnik.bat
   Pervyy zapusk 1-3 minuty (zagruzka modeley).
   Kogda poyavitsya zelenoe "Gotovo" — mozhno sprashivat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
NUZHNO NA KOMPYUTERE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Windows 64-bit
- Videokarta NVIDIA + drayver s nvidia.com
  (edinstvennoe, chto nelzya polozhit v papku)
- Svobodno ~6+ GB videopamyati (zakroyte brauzer, esli tormozit)

Python, modeli i llama.cpp UZHE vnutri papki — stavit nichego ne nado.
